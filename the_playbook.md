# The AI-Powered SEO Content Playbook

**Research Topic:** How practitioners use AI to scale SEO content production without sacrificing quality.

## Section 1: Recommendations (w/ Source Citations)

### Step 1 Master the Fundamentals Before Adding AI

Traditional SEO fundamentals have not changed. People still search using keywords and topics, content still needs to be findable and indexable, and backlinks still help pages rank. Google still receives 5 trillion searches per year, roughly 100x more conversations than ChatGPT is expected to have this year. (source: Ahrefs, AhrefsCom, YouTube; "SEO in 2025: How I'd Learn it if I Were Starting Over"; 9 April 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE))

Separately, according to a 2025 study by Higher Visibility, 79.8% of Americans still prefer traditional search engines for their queries while AI search is rising, but hasn't gone mainstream yet. (source: Higher Visibility, 2025 study, cited by Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))

Strong traditional SEO is the foundation for AI search visibility. You likely won't appear in AI-generated answers if you don't first rank well organically. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is less a new discipline to master and more an additional layer added onto an existing SEO foundation. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))

**What this means in practice:**

- Fix technical and UX issues, identify keyword and backlink gaps, and match content to search intent before reaching for AI tools. (source: Nathan Gotch, LinkedIn; 16 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nathangotch_seo-is-so-easy-now-you-just-need-to-share-7472611026111434752-qWzJ/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nathangotch_seo-is-so-easy-now-you-just-need-to-share-7472611026111434752-qWzJ/))
- Don't skip fundamentals because AI tools seem to replace them, as the quality of AI output is only as good as the SEO knowledge guiding it. Someone without SEO expertise doesn't guide AI; AI guides them. (source: Ahrefs, AhrefsCom, YouTube; "SEO in 2025: How I'd Learn it if I Were Starting Over"; 9 April 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE))

### Step 2 Shift Your Mindset: Users and Agents First, Algorithms Second

The old mechanical approach to copy top-ranking pages, add keywords, get backlinks that no longer works because AI can mass-produce that kind of content at scale, and Google is responding by cracking down on it. (source: Ahrefs, AhrefsCom, YouTube; "SEO in 2025: How I'd Learn it if I Were Starting Over"; 9 April 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE)) Ranking for individual keywords is no longer a strategy. (source: Rand Fishkin, LinkedIn; 5 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/randfishkin_the-death-of-clicks-rand-fishkin-on-brand-activity-7468575767635890176-znz0/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/randfishkin_the-death-of-clicks-rand-fishkin-on-brand-activity-7468575767635890176-znz0/))

Replace keyword-obsession with a user-obsessed mindset: understand what the searcher actually wants to know, why they want it, and how they want the information delivered. (source: Ahrefs, AhrefsCom, YouTube; "SEO in 2025: How I'd Learn it if I Were Starting Over"; 9 April 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE)) Beyond human users, AI agents are now acting as shoppers and researchers while high-intent purchase journeys are collapsing from 12+ steps to 3 or fewer, and brands that don't optimize for AI as a primary customer get filtered out. (source: AJ Ghergich, LinkedIn; 11 July 2025; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ajghergich_my-late-grandmother-always-told-people-i-share-7349440790181367808-k_I7/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ajghergich_my-late-grandmother-always-told-people-i-share-7349440790181367808-k_I7/)); (source: Neil Patel, LinkedIn; 17 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/neilkpatel_aimarketing-futureofseo-searchengine-ugcPost-7472709760400846848-XNAb/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/neilkpatel_aimarketing-futureofseo-searchengine-ugcPost-7472709760400846848-XNAb/))

Google's MUM model connects keywords into "journeys" and identifies search intent across a full path, not a single query. Entity-first indexing and understanding language-agnostic concepts rather than just keywords which drives knowledge graph results and AI responses. (source: Cindy Krum, LinkedIn; 23 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cindykrum_seo-googlesearch-mum-ugcPost-7475095892191105025-SRcK/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cindykrum_seo-googlesearch-mum-ugcPost-7475095892191105025-SRcK/))

### Step 3 How to use AI as an Assistant, Not a Replacement

AI tools are faster and better than most practitioners at brainstorming, writing, and analyzing data. But quality of output is only as good as the guidance they receive. The right model is: do keyword research and audience research yourself, then feed findings into AI to execute on strategy. (source: Ahrefs, AhrefsCom, YouTube; "SEO in 2025: How I'd Learn it if I Were Starting Over"; 9 April 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE))

A proven content production workflow using this model (source: Nathan Gotch, LinkedIn; 6 May 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nathangotch_when-creating-pages-for-seo-aeo-they-activity-7457790873058291712-1MuE](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nathangotch_when-creating-pages-for-seo-aeo-they-activity-7457790873058291712-1MuE)):

1. **Build a Search Brainiac**  Data layer (GSC, GA4, AI search tracking), Company layer (products, FAQs), Expertise layer (founder/SME interviews, user-generated content)
2. **Create a Swipe File**  Study top competitors, convert their best content into templates, repeat for ~100 assets
3. **Research the Right Way**  Identify top competitors for your target keyword from both traditional search and AI citations; extract entities and content structure
4. **Perform a Page-Level Gap Analysis**  Use ChatGPT to identify topic gaps versus top competitors
5. **Build a Purple Cow Content Strategy**  Use what works, what's relevant, and identified gaps to create a differentiated content angle
6. **Write a Killer Content Brief**  Include intelligence from your search brainiac, at least 3 proven templates matching the content type, the most important entities to cover, and your differentiation angle

Whether you use AI or a human writer to execute from that brief is secondary. The brief is what prevents generic output. (source: Nathan Gotch, LinkedIn; 6 May 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nathangotch_when-creating-pages-for-seo-aeo-they-activity-7457790873058291712-1MuE](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nathangotch_when-creating-pages-for-seo-aeo-they-activity-7457790873058291712-1MuE))

### Step 4 Fix Intent Alignment Before Creating New Content

Most underperforming pages suffer from intent mismatch, not thin content. (source: Matt Diggity, LinkedIn; 15 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/)) Adding new content before fixing existing pages compounds the problem.

**What this means in practice:**

- Refresh existing content to match what users actually need from each query
- Improve structure, readability, and depth on informational pages
- Build contextual internal links from educational content to commercial/conversion pages
- Run a full user-journey audit to find friction points and weak CTAs
(source: Matt Diggity, LinkedIn; 15 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/))

Also audit for keyword and topic cannibalization. When multiple pages target the same intent, authority is split between them and none rank effectively. Consolidate or differentiate overlapping pages so each holds a distinct focus. (source: Matt Diggity, LinkedIn; 15 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/))

Case evidence: In a trust-sensitive eCommerce niche, fixing intent alignment as part of a broader 5-step strategy drove +59.6% YoY organic sessions and AI Overviews visibility from near zero to 634 high-intent keywords. (source: Matt Diggity, LinkedIn; 15 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/))

### Step 5 How to Structure Content So AI Can Parse and Repeat It

Generative AI platforms often pull from top-ranking search results and trusted sources to compose answers. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I)) To be the source they pull from, content must be structured for machine consumption, not just human readers.

**What AI-ready content looks like:**

- **Natural language and question-focused writing** The AI models are built on conversational text and naturally prefer content that sounds like a human expert explaining something. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- **Clear heading hierarchy** When an AI is asked a specific question, a proper heading directs it to the right section. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- **Bullet points and numbered lists** Is easy for AI to quote verbatim, key points don't get lost in paragraphs. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- **FAQ sections** Capture succinct Q&A pairs that AI can parse and repeat directly. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- **Tables for comparative data** AI can parse individual cells and may quote specific values. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- **Semantic richness** Use a natural variety of related terms, concepts, and entities rather than repeating the same keyword. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))

Well-organized, authoritative content with clear sections and FAQ entries increased inclusion in AI-generated answers by up to 37% on platforms like Perplexity (source: 2024 study, cited by Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I)).

**E-E-A-T signals to include in content:**

- Firsthand experiences or case studies
- Statistics and citations from reputable sources
- Author credentials visible on every piece
- Multiple perspectives on complex topics
- Addresses potential objections or limitations
(source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))

### Step 6 Handle Technical Foundations for AI Crawlability

AI search tools primarily read raw HTML. Content hidden behind JavaScript interactions or embedded in images may be missed entirely. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I)) A 1,200% jump in AI-driven retail visits occurred between July 2024 and February 2025 brands that were invisible to AI missed all of it. (source: AJ Ghergich, LinkedIn; 11 July 2025; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ajghergich_my-late-grandmother-always-told-people-i-share-7349440790181367808-k_I7/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ajghergich_my-late-grandmother-always-told-people-i-share-7349440790181367808-k_I7/))

**Technical checklist:**

- **robots.txt** Confirm you are not blocking OpenAI's GPTBot, Google's crawlers, or Microsoft's Bingbot. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- **HTML text, not JavaScript** Key information must be in raw HTML, not rendered via JavaScript interactions. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- **Alt text and transcripts** Descriptive alt text for images, transcripts for videos make multimedia content accessible to AI. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- **Bing indexing and sitemap submission** ChatGPT Search uses Bing's index, not Google's. Submit a sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools and confirm indexing there. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I)); (source: AJ Ghergich, LinkedIn; 11 July 2025; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ajghergich_my-late-grandmother-always-told-people-i-share-7349440790181367808-k_I7/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ajghergich_my-late-grandmother-always-told-people-i-share-7349440790181367808-k_I7/))
- **Site structure** Most valuable content should be within a few clicks of the homepage; use descriptive internal linking. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- **Structured product feeds** For eCommerce, structured product feeds are the sitemaps of agentic commerce; foundational and protocol-agnostic regardless of which AI standard wins. (source: AJ Ghergich, LinkedIn; 17 April 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ajghergich_agenticcommerce-aisearch-seo-share-7450597739681222657-U67n/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ajghergich_agenticcommerce-aisearch-seo-share-7450597739681222657-U67n/))

**Schema markup:**

- Organization schema tells AI about your brand, name, logo, and social profiles, with the basis of knowledge panels. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- Article schema signals publication date, author, and headline that factors AI uses to determine recency and credibility. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- Google has confirmed there is no secret AI tag, schema types already used in standard SEO practice are what AI relies on. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))

**Off-site structured data:** Add your business to Wikidata (the structured database behind Wikipedia), Google My Business, Bing Places, LinkedIn Company Pages, Crunchbase, and any relevant industry databases. AI models sample data across large knowledge sources, and these are prime candidates for LLM training data. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))

### Step 7 Build Off-Property Authority in the Specific Topics AI Associates With You

AI reuses trust already attached to sources it pulls from, it doesn't form a fresh opinion about your brand on every query. It favors entities it already recognizes on a specific topic. Your owned blog is one of the weaker inputs, publications, analysts, and competitors that mention you carry more weight because the model doesn't have to take your word for your own authority. (source: Kevin Indig, LinkedIn; 16 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_two-brands-can-run-identical-on-page-optimization-share-7472341555127926784-La0z/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_two-brands-can-run-identical-on-page-optimization-share-7472341555127926784-La0z/))

AI trusts a different set of sources for every topic, so there is no fixed list. Off-property authority has to be topic-specific. For example, competitor domains hold 33.5% of what AI cites for invoicing questions, but only 7% for starting a business questions. Building mentions everywhere wastes budget. (source: Kevin Indig, LinkedIn; 15 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_ai-trusts-a-different-set-of-sources-for-share-7472249743503503360-3b4H/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_ai-trusts-a-different-set-of-sources-for-share-7472249743503503360-3b4H/))

The data reinforces this: 75% of AI citations pull from sources outside Google's top 10. Ranking #1 in Google still only gives a 31% shot at appearing in AI answers. Brands now have two scores that matter, and most are only tracking one. (source: Neil Patel, LinkedIn; 16 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/neilkpatel_seo-aimarketing-googlesearch-ugcPost-7472619214919630848-pPyi/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/neilkpatel_seo-aimarketing-googlesearch-ugcPost-7472619214919630848-pPyi/))

Branded web mentions correlate with AI visibility at 0.66–0.71 the strongest signal in the Ahrefs dataset. (source: Ahrefs, "10 SEO Trends I've Seen Firsthand in 2026 (With Data)"; https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-trends/)

**What to do:**

- Map the specific domains AI already cites for your topic, then earn your way into that set. (source: Kevin Indig, LinkedIn; 15 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_ai-trusts-a-different-set-of-sources-for-share-7472249743503503360-3b4H/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_ai-trusts-a-different-set-of-sources-for-share-7472249743503503360-3b4H/))
- Secure brand mentions and editorial links from relevant industry publications. (source: Matt Diggity, LinkedIn; 15 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/))
- Proactively pitch inclusion in "best of" roundups on authoritative sites, even a mention without a dofollow link is valuable because LLMs sample that data. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))
- Build community-driven visibility on Reddit. In trust-sensitive niches, buyers check Reddit before they check Google, and AI tools pull heavily from those conversations. (source: Matt Diggity, LinkedIn; 15 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/))
- Stack E-E-A-T and Digital PR: expand About pages with real credentials, make author information visible on every piece, implement "Why You Can Trust Us" sections. (source: Matt Diggity, LinkedIn; 15 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mattdiggityseo_how-we-outranked-the-original-manufacturer-ugcPost-7472295350175895554-3rUy/))

Case evidence: Queensmith (London jeweller) secured editorial coverage in The Telegraph, Grazia, and Marie Claire, with tripled site visitors and exceeded $1M average monthly revenue within 8 months. Examine.com, a nutrition database staffed by PhD researchers, gets natural backlinks from the NYT, Guardian, and Forbes with zero outreach, and recovered from a 90% traffic drop after a 2018 Google update. (source: SurferSEO, "12 Real Life SEO Examples to Learn From"; https://surferseo.com/blog/seo-examples/)

### Step 8 Develop Comprehensive Topical Authority

AI Overviews mention an average of 5 sources per query, and 90% of the time Google lists 8 or fewer sources, but multiple pieces of content from the same domain can appear. A site that covers a topic from every angle significantly increases its chances of being cited multiple times. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))

AI search engines also use Query Fan-Out: they generate multiple hidden sub-queries from one user prompt. A single page targeting one keyword won't capture this. Topic clusters that are not individual pages, are the structural response. Interest in this topic is up +2,550%. (source: Ahrefs, "10 SEO Trends I've Seen Firsthand in 2026 (With Data)"; https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-trends/)

Information-only sites are down approximately 72% from peak traffic because AI Overviews now directly answer most informational queries. The sites that survive and grow use content to build authority toward commercial outcomes, not to answer generic questions in isolation. (source: Ahrefs, "10 SEO Trends I've Seen Firsthand in 2026 (With Data)"; https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-trends/) Surfer SEO's content-led approach, with educational content covering the full niche, not just product keywords and 11 times their organic traffic in two years and attributed approximately 25% of company revenue to content. (source: SurferSEO, "12 Real Life SEO Examples to Learn From"; https://surferseo.com/blog/seo-examples/)

### Step 9 Distribute Across Platforms; Visibility on Each Feeds AI Answers

Traffic is fragmenting across YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, and Amazon. Visibility on these platforms now feeds AI answer visibility too. (source: Ahrefs, "10 SEO Trends I've Seen Firsthand in 2026 (With Data)"; https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-trends/) SEO skills with keyword research, search intent, link building, technical fundamentals, can transfer to any platform that surfaces content based on user queries. (source: Ahrefs, AhrefsCom, YouTube; "SEO in 2025: How I'd Learn it if I Were Starting Over"; 9 April 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_s2h7X-c2jE))

Google, TikTok, and Facebook are processing video audio and visual data in real-time. Optimizing for AI comprehension of video content means paying close attention to what happens within videos, the AI is watching and listening, not just reading text around it. (source: Cindy Krum, LinkedIn; 25 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cindykrum_seo-ai-videomarketing-ugcPost-7475818667163799552-Woxy/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cindykrum_seo-ai-videomarketing-ugcPost-7475818667163799552-Woxy/))

Rand Fishkin's framing: the shift is from traffic-driven to influence-driven marketing. PR remains one of the most undervalued marketing channels, with presence across the sources your audience actually reads, watches, and listens to creates compounding visibility that AI citation directly benefits from. (source: Rand Fishkin, LinkedIn; 5 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/randfishkin_the-death-of-clicks-rand-fishkin-on-brand-activity-7468575767635890176-znz0/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/randfishkin_the-death-of-clicks-rand-fishkin-on-brand-activity-7468575767635890176-znz0/))

### Step 10 Track AI Visibility, Not Just Rankings

AI Overviews reduce clicks by 58% and Google is keeping more users on the SERP. The unit economics of SEO have fundamentally changed. (source: Ahrefs, "10 SEO Trends I've Seen Firsthand in 2026 (With Data)"; https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-trends/) Tracking clicks from LLMs is often not useful because users get answers conversationally and don't click. The right metric right now is brand mentions by AI, not click-through. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I))

Two scores now matter: traditional search rankings and AI citation rate. Most brands are only tracking one. (source: Neil Patel, LinkedIn; 16 June 2026; [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/neilkpatel_seo-aimarketing-googlesearch-ugcPost-7472619214919630848-pPyi/](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/neilkpatel_seo-aimarketing-googlesearch-ugcPost-7472619214919630848-pPyi/)) Branded web mentions correlate with AI visibility at 0.66–0.71 and monitoring those mentions is currently the most reliable leading indicator. (source: Ahrefs, "10 SEO Trends I've Seen Firsthand in 2026 (With Data)"; https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-trends/)

## Section 2: Where Experts Disagree

### Disagreement 1 How urgent is the shift to AI search, really?

**AJ Ghergich** argues the shift is already here and brands that don't act now will lose ground permanently. He points to a 1,200% jump in AI-driven retail visits between July 2024 and February 2025, describes purchase journeys collapsing from 12+ steps to 3 or fewer, and frames this as "a completely new game" where brands need to win at AI-driven retrieval before anything else. (source: AJ Ghergich, LinkedIn; 11 July 2025; https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ajghergich_my-late-grandmother-always-told-people-i-share-7349440790181367808-k_I7/)

**Matt Kenyon (Surfer SEO)** takes a more measured position. A 2025 Higher Visibility study found 79.8% of Americans still prefer traditional search engines, and he frames GEO as "less of a new discipline you have to master and more of an additional layer" added onto an existing SEO foundation, not a replacement requiring urgent pivot. (source: Matt Kenyon, Surfer SEO, YouTube; "How to Dominate AI Search Results in 2026"; 28 May 2025; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhTo8fDmr5I)

**Side I take:** Kenyon's framing, with a caveat. Ghergich's data is real, but it comes from e-commerce and fashion retail specifically, categories where AI shopping agents are furthest along. For content-driven SEO generally, Kenyon's "additional layer, not a new discipline" framing matches what shows up across the rest of the research and the tactics that improve AI visibility (structure, EEAT, topical authority) are largely the same tactics that already work for classic SEO. I'd tell a team to build this into existing workflows as a near-term priority, not treat it as a five-alarm fire that needs a separate budget line.

---

### Disagreement 2 Should you scale AI content production aggressively, or hold the line on depth?

**Nathan Gotch** describes a scale-oriented workflow: build a "swipe file" by studying top competitors and converting their best content into a reusable template, then repeat that process for roughly 100 assets. (source: Nathan Gotch, LinkedIn; 6 May 2026; https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nathangotch_when-creating-pages-for-seo-aeo-they-activity-7457790873058291712-1MuE)

**SurferSEO's own case study data** argues against volume as a strategy on its own. HubSpot lost 80% of its traffic after publishing large volumes of off-topic content (quote templates, generic tools) purely to maximize traffic surface area, following Google's November/December 2024 core updates. Tailride published 22,000 AI-generated pages targeting unrelated long-tail keywords; traffic spiked, then crashed three months later once Google's spam policies caught up. (source: SurferSEO, "12 Real Life SEO Examples to Learn From"; https://surferseo.com/blog/seo-examples/)

**Side I take:** The case study data, but the disagreement is softer than it first looks. Gotch's own post doesn't actually argue for volume without quality control, his workflow ties every one of those 100 assets to competitor research and a gap analysis first, and he explicitly builds in a "content brief" step specifically to prevent generic output. The real disagreement is about how much scale is safe once quality controls exist. The case studies suggest the failure mode isn't AI content itself, it's AI content published without topical relevance or editorial review. I'd cap early-stage scale at whatever a small team can genuinely fact-check and edit, and treat "100 assets" as a target to grow into once the review process is proven, not a week-one target.

### Disagreement 3 Is it worth investing in new AI-specific technical formats now?

**Ahrefs' own data** is skeptical of at least one popular new format: their analysis found 97% of llms.txt files get zero visits and no major AI provider actually uses the file, concluding it's "largely useless" and not worth the time. (source: Ahrefs, "10 SEO Trends I've Seen Firsthand in 2026 (With Data)"; https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-trends/)

**AJ Ghergich**, writing about agentic commerce, argues the opposite direction for a related category of new technical infrastructure: structured, machine-readable product feeds are "the sitemaps of agentic commerce," foundational and protocol-agnostic, and brands that invest early "compound their advantage no matter which standard wins." (source: AJ Ghergich, LinkedIn; 17 April 2026; https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ajghergich_agenticcommerce-aisearch-seo-share-7450597739681222657-U67n/)

**Side I take:** Both, because they're not actually describing the same artifact. llms.txt is a single speculative file with no confirmed adoption by any AI provider, skip it. Structured product feeds and schema markup are different: they're built on standards search engines and AI crawlers already demonstrably use (see Step 5 and Step 6 above). The generalizable rule here is really Ahrefs' own standard: invest in machine-readability that has evidence of being read, and skip formats that exist mainly as a trend.
